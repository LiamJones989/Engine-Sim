The four strokes (cylinder 1)

  1. Intake. The piston falls and the intake valve opens. Air and fuel flow in past the throttle.
  2. Compression. Both valves shut and the piston rises, squeezing the mixture.
  3. Power. The spark ignites the mixture. Hot expanding gas shoves the piston down.
  4. Exhaust. The exhaust valve opens and the rising piston pushes burnt gas out.
